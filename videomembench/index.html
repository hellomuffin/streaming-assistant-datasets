How a dialogue is evaluated

    
Protocol and metrics (from docs/PROTOCOL.md in the VideoMemBench GitHub repository)
    

      
This is an offline, multi-turn benchmark. It is not a streaming benchmark: the whole video is visible from the first turn, and the turns carry no timestamps. What it tests is whether the model keeps track of what was established earlier in the conversation.

      
    
        
  1. Visual input: 128 frames sampled uniformly over the whole video, each labelled with its <MM:SS> time, resized to at most 512 px. No audio and no subtitles. The 128 sample positions are drawn on the timeline below.
    2. 
        
  2. Self-rollout history: at turn t the model sees V || (q1, â1) || … || (qt−1, ât−1) || qt. The â are the model's own earlier answers, not the references, so an early mistake stays in the context.
    3. 
        
  3. Episodic dependency: the later question revisits a scene, entity or event set up in an earlier turn ("the woman from T7"). Answer dependency: the later question builds on the earlier answer.
    4. 
        
  4. Grading: multiple choice by extracted letter (A–H); open answers by a GPT-5.4 mini judge for semantic equivalence; true/false by the last yes/no token.
    5. 
        
  5. TurnAcc = accuracy over all turns. EpiDepAcc = accuracy over turns with an episodic parent; the parent need not be right. AnsDepAcc counts a turn only if it and every turn reachable through answer edges are right. The "answer chain" column below lists those turns. Overall = mean of the three.
