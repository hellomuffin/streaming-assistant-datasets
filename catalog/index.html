Streaming Assistant Training Data Catalog public data for training real-time visual interaction models

The question this page answers

If you want to train a visual streaming interaction model (a real-time assistant that watches video, stays quiet, and speaks at the right moment to guide a task, correct a mistake, answer a question or raise an alert), which public datasets can you use, how large are they, what is in the videos, and what annotations come with each video?

We checked 153 datasets against their live download pages, cards and papers on 16–17 Sep 2026. Where the annotation files were open and reasonably small we downloaded them and counted the numbers ourselves; those figures are tagged (computed). Click any dataset name below to open its full entry: scale, what people do, the list of annotations per video with who made them, a verbatim annotation excerpt, license, caveats and sources.

Three terms used throughout

Ready-made interaction data already contains assistant turns placed on the video timeline (and usually silence), so you can train on it directly. Source video has human temporal labels (steps, actions, mistakes, events) but no assistant turns; you convert it by deciding when the assistant speaks and generating what it says. Timing source is where the "speak now" moment comes from: a real human who chose to speak (the gold standard), a human annotation boundary copied by a rule, an ASR word time, or an LLM/VLM decision.

The single most important fact about the whole landscape: in almost every large public set, the moment the assistant speaks is derived, not observed. It is copied from a step boundary (ProAssist, GuideMe, Pro2Bench), chosen by an LLM (Ego-CoMist, Ambient, JoyAI), or taken from a narrator's ASR (LiveCC). Only a handful of sets record when a real person chose to guide or correct someone, and they are small (see "Real human guidance" below).

5 · What does not exist publicly (the gaps)

  • No large dataset of real, timed mistake corrections. Real corrections total a few thousand (HoloAssist ≈4k verbal corrections, WTaG 207, QICD ≈1.4k post-error feedbacks). The only 10k+ mistake set, Ego-CoMist, is counterfactual text over correct video.
  • No released large-scale egocentric speak/silent training set with human-verified timing. EgoProactive (9,935 decisions) is validation-sized; Pro2Bench's 250k-clip training set and GuideMe-Train are unreleased.
  • No omni (audio + video) duplex interaction data from the leading models is released. MiniCPM-o 4.5, Qwen3-Omni, Baichuan-Omni-1.5, LongCat and VITA publish recipes only; Gander released 12 example dialogues of an announced 2.7M.
  • No dataset shows the user reacting to the assistant's correction. Every set is recorded or labelled open-loop: the video does not change because of what the assistant said (HoloAssist, WTaG and Ego-EXTRA come closest because the guidance was live).
  • Video availability is eroding. Most web sets ship YouTube IDs only: COIN test is down to 2,354 of 2,797 videos, CrossTask 3,675 of 4,700; Live-WhisperX clips were deleted from HF on 2026-08-12; HowTo100M and ViTT official links return 403.

6 · A practical mixture (our recommendation, not a published recipe)

  1. Streaming mechanics pre-training: Live-WhisperX-526K or Inf-Streams-Train for talking in sync with frames; MMDuet2 and Streamo-style per-second silence; JoyAI alerts, time-aligned QA and counting families.
  2. Egocentric task guidance SFT: ProAssist (backbone), MMDuet2 ego, EgoProactive + Ambient labels in the same 8-s speak/silent format, converted Ego4D Goal-Step / EgoExoLearn / COIN step onsets.
  3. Mistakes and corrections: HoloAssist raw instructor corrections (the most faithful real signal), Assembly101 mistake/correction boundaries, CaptainCook4D errors (via QICD timing), Ego-CoMist counterfactuals (with the instruction in context), IMPACT / EPIC-Tent / IndEgo for non-kitchen errors.
  4. Real-timing calibration and evaluation: fine-tune the speak head on HoloAssist, Ego-EXTRA and WTaG, whose timing comes from real people, and hold out Ego-MC-Bench, EgoProactive (hidden test via the challenge) and EgoServe for evaluation.
  5. If the model hears and speaks: InteractSpeech timelines (barge-in, backchannel) with TTS, Seamless Interaction for human turn timing, Ego4D AV-social / EgoCom / MMCSG for "is this speech addressed to me".

Licenses differ sharply: Ego-CoMist, QICD and QEVD are Qualcomm research licenses; EPIC-KITCHENS, Assembly101, Ego-EXTRA, Seamless Interaction are non-commercial; Ego4D and Ego-Exo4D allow commercial model training but forbid redistributing the data; HoloAssist (CDLA-Permissive-2.0), CaptainCook4D (Apache-2.0), IndEgo (CC-BY-4.0) and ProAssist/JoyAI annotations (Apache-2.0) are the most permissive. Pick data by the behaviour you want the model to learn

Each card names the capability, the best public data for it (real human timing first), the larger but synthetic options, and how people convert source labels into training turns. Click a dataset name to open its entry.

1 · When to speak and when to stay silent

Real human timing: HoloAssist (instructor utterance times), Ego-EXTRA (pro-active vs on-demand experts), WTaG, QEVD FitCoach.

Explicit silence labels at scale: EgoProactive (9,935 decisions), Ambient (26k rows), MMDuet2 (NO REPLY every 2 s), Streamo (Silence/Standby/Response), SOVBench-T (balanced trigger vs never-appears).

Watch out: narration sets (LiveCC ≈99% voiced) teach the model to talk constantly; macro-F1 on fixed 8-s intervals can be gamed by cadence alone.

2 · Step-by-step task guidance

Ready-made: ProAssist (1.47M turns on six ego corpora), GoalStep LiveChat, Proact-VL Ego4D coaching, QICD instructions and successes.

Convert from step labels: Ego4D Goal-Step, EgoExoLearn, Ego-Exo4D keysteps, ENIGMA-360 (steps were actually spoken by an app), COIN, HT-Step, HowToCaption (imperative step text).

Recipe: instruction at step start, confirmation at step end, silence between.

3 · Detecting and correcting mistakes

Real errors with real corrections: HoloAssist (7,562 wrong actions, 4,052 corrected verbally, linked), Assembly101 (mistake → correction segments), IMPACT (anomaly → recovery), WTaG (207 corrections).

Scripted or typed errors: CaptainCook4D (2,574 tags), EgoPER, EPIC-Tent, IndustReal, EgoOops (free-text descriptions), IndEgo (industrial, step-level).

Large synthetic: Ego-CoMist (25k counterfactual pairs), ProAssist correction turns (43k). Human-timed post-error feedback: QICD.

4 · Answering the user's questions at the right time

Real spoken questions on glasses video: EgoConv (4,415 questions in natural multi-party settings), EgoBlind (5,311 blind users' questions).

Backward / present / forward timing: JoyAI (1.75M time-aligned QA records), TimeChat-Online-139K, ESTP-IT (answer windows), EgoTimeQA (303k QA with evidence windows), RealTimeVideo-112K.

Multi-turn memory: SVBench, CogStream, StreamingQA-120K.

5 · Proactive alerts ("tell me when …") and hazards

Ready-made: ROMA alerts (27k), JoyAI alerting family (247k), ViSpeak anomaly warnings.

Human onset labels to convert: OOPS! (moment an action goes wrong), UCF-Crime + UCA, HIVAU-70k (13,251 anomaly events with explanations), XD-Violence, DoTA and MM-AU (dashcam, warn before collision), DiDeMo, Charades-STA, QVHighlights, TimeLens-100K.

Assistive hazards: VIABench (13,152 human alert windows), WAD.

6 · Live commentary and narration

Real narrators at scale: Live-WhisperX-526K (14k h), Live-CC-5M, Inf-Streams-Train (full games), VidChapters-7M, HTM-AA.

Sparse, event-triggered speech with real silences: MatchTime (soccer, re-timed), SoccerReplay-1988, BoxComm (≈34% silent), RacingGame (≈56% silent), MAD (audio description between dialogue).

Egocentric play-by-play: Ego4D narrations (3.85M sentences), Narration Stream, OmniStar-RNG.

7 · Counting and progress over time

RepCount-A (per-repetition boundaries, 15,589 reps), Countix, JoyAI counting family (141k: count every n s, wait N s, duration), VideoChatOnline-IT ("steps completed so far"), EgoEMS CPR rate/depth traces, FineGym element boundaries.

8 · Spoken turn-taking: barge-in, backchannel, addressee

Timelines: InteractSpeech (59k dialogues, 76k backchannels, 14.7k interruptions; audio via TTS), Gander examples (listen/speak/interrupt unit format).

Real human conversation video: Seamless Interaction (64,751 dyadic conversations, 4,000+ h), CANDOR (850+ h), Ego4D Talking-to-Me, EgoCom, MMCSG, EasyCom.

Visual wake-up / interruption: ViSpeak-Instruct (people wave, point, interrupt on camera).

9 · Long-horizon memory and reminders

EgoLife (6 people × 7 days, 358k caption lines, 301k transcript lines), EgoServe (task reminders, habit coaching, memory recall triggers), EgoLongQA, HD-EPIC (object itineraries), MultiHop-EgoQA, Ego4D NLQ / episodic memory.

10 · Coaching physical technique

Ego-Exo4D expert commentary + tips (117.8k comments; cooking, bike repair, sports, music), QEVD FitCoach (live coach feedback), EgoExo-Fitness (keypoint violations + advice), Fitness-AQA (natural form errors, clip-level).
