How records were assigned to the six families

    
Classification rules (applied to every record of the full release; first matching rule wins)
    

      
The release has four task_type values (chat, event_grounding, narration, background), not six families. The paper (§3.2) defines the families but gives no labels, so each record was labelled from its wording, its source and the timing of its responses. The rules are heuristics written by the viewer author, not labels from JD. A record that mixes several behaviours (for example casual questions plus a counting instruction) gets the first family below that matches.
